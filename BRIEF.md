# Cardio Explorer — Product Brief

**Cardio Explorer** is an interactive heart health learning web app for students. It turns the heart, blood flow and heart-health habits into things students can click, test and explore.

## Problem Statement
Students often struggle to visualize cardiovascular anatomy, blood flow, and how daily habits affect biometric indicators like heart rate. Textbook diagrams are static, blood flow is hard to picture as a loop, and the link between everyday choices and numbers like heart rate feels abstract.

## Target Audience
Middle and high school students learning human biology and health, and the teachers who guide them.

## Core Features
1. **Interactive 4-Chamber Heart & Blood Flow Explorer:** select each chamber and valve to learn its job, and follow blood through the heart, lungs and body.
2. **Heart Rate Zones & Pulse Simulator:** learn to take a pulse and see how heart rate changes from rest through light, moderate and hard activity.
3. **Daily Habits & Cardiovascular Risk Quiz:** short questions on food, activity, sleep, stress and tobacco, with instant explanations of how each habit affects the heart.
4. **Quick Heart Health Glossary & Fact Cards:** plain-language definitions and flip-style fact cards for key terms, for quick review before a test.

## Success Metrics
- **Clear visual engagement:** every feature is interactive and visual, not just text.
- **Responsive layout:** works on phones, tablets, Chromebooks and desktops, with no sideways scrolling.
- **Accurate educational content:** facts are checked against reputable sources (for example WHO and the American Heart Association), cited where statistics are used, and framed as education, not medical advice.

## Out of Scope (for now)
User accounts, saving progress, teacher dashboards and personal health tracking or diagnosis.
